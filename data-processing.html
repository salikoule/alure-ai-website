Data Processing Agreement

How we process personal data on behalf of our customers

Last Updated: January 1, 2024

1. Introduction

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the Terms of Service between you (the "Customer" or "Data Controller") and Alure.ai Inc. ("Alure.ai," "we," "us," or "Data Processor") regarding the processing of personal data.

This DPA applies when Alure.ai processes personal data on behalf of the Customer in the course of providing our AI-powered call script generation and sales automation services.

Key Definitions

Personal Data: Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
Data Controller: The entity that determines the purposes and means of processing personal data.
Data Processor: The entity that processes personal data on behalf of the Data Controller.
Data Subject: The individual to whom the personal data relates.

2. Scope and Applicability

2.1 When This DPA Applies

This DPA applies when:

  • You use our services to process personal data of prospects, customers, or other individuals
  • The data subjects are located in jurisdictions with data protection laws (EU, UK, California, etc.)
  • You act as a Data Controller and we act as a Data Processor

2.2 Data Processing Relationship

In most cases:

  • You (Customer) are the Data Controller for prospect and customer data
  • Alure.ai is the Data Processor providing services on your behalf
  • You determine what personal data to process and for what purposes
  • We process the data according to your instructions and this DPA

3. Categories of Data and Processing Activities

Data Category Examples Processing Purpose
Prospect Data Names, job titles, company information, contact details Script generation, research automation
Communication Records Call logs, email interactions, meeting notes Performance tracking, optimization
Professional Information LinkedIn profiles, work history, company details Personalization, research enhancement
Behavioral Data Response rates, engagement metrics, preferences AI training, script improvement

3.1 Data Subjects

The personal data we process may relate to:

  • Business prospects and leads
  • Existing customers and clients
  • Professional contacts
  • Decision makers and influencers

4. Your Instructions and Our Processing

4.1 Processing Instructions

We will process personal data only:

  • According to your documented instructions
  • As necessary to provide our services
  • As required by applicable law
  • As specified in this DPA and our Terms of Service

4.2 Lawful Instructions

You warrant that your instructions:

  • Comply with applicable data protection laws
  • Do not require us to process data unlawfully
  • Are within the scope of our services
  • Include necessary consent or legal basis for processing

4.3 Conflicting Instructions

If we believe your instructions conflict with applicable law, we will:

  • Inform you immediately
  • Suspend the conflicting processing
  • Work with you to resolve the issue
  • Document any legal requirements that prevent compliance

5. Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data:

Technical Safeguards

  • End-to-end encryption (AES-256)
  • Secure data transmission (TLS 1.3)
  • Regular security assessments and penetration testing
  • Multi-factor authentication
  • Intrusion detection and prevention systems

Organizational Safeguards

  • Role-based access controls
  • Employee training on data protection
  • Confidentiality agreements for all staff
  • Regular security awareness programs
  • Incident response procedures

Physical Safeguards

  • Secure data centers with 24/7 monitoring
  • Biometric access controls
  • Environmental controls and backup systems
  • Secure disposal of storage media

6. Sub-Processors

6.1 Current Sub-Processors

We use the following sub-processors to provide our services:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): Cloud hosting and infrastructure
  • Google Cloud Platform: AI and machine learning services
  • Stripe: Payment processing
  • SendGrid: Email delivery services
  • Intercom: Customer support and communication

6.2 Sub-Processor Requirements

All sub-processors must:

  • Provide appropriate security guarantees
  • Be bound by data protection obligations
  • Comply with this DPA and applicable laws
  • Undergo regular security assessments

6.3 Changes to Sub-Processors

We will:

  • Notify you at least 30 days before adding new sub-processors
  • Provide information about the new sub-processor
  • Allow you to object to the addition
  • Work with you to address reasonable objections

7. International Data Transfers

7.1 Transfer Mechanisms

When transferring personal data internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards through:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission
  • Adequacy decisions by relevant authorities
  • Binding Corporate Rules (where applicable)
  • Other approved transfer mechanisms

7.2 Data Locations

Personal data may be processed in:

  • United States (primary data centers)
  • European Union (for EU customers)
  • Other locations as required for service delivery

8. Your Rights and Obligations

8.1 Data Subject Rights Support

We will assist you in responding to data subject rights requests, including:

  • Access: Providing copies of personal data
  • Rectification: Correcting inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Deleting personal data when required
  • Portability: Exporting data in a structured format
  • Restriction: Limiting processing activities
  • Objection: Stopping certain processing activities

8.2 Your Responsibilities

As the Data Controller, you must:

  • Have a lawful basis for processing personal data
  • Provide appropriate privacy notices to data subjects
  • Obtain necessary consents
  • Respond to data subject rights requests
  • Ensure accuracy of data provided to us
  • Comply with applicable data protection laws

9. Data Breaches

9.1 Incident Response

In the event of a personal data breach, we will:

  • Notify you without undue delay (within 24 hours when possible)
  • Provide details of the nature and scope of the breach
  • Describe measures taken to address the breach
  • Recommend steps to mitigate potential harm
  • Provide regular updates as the investigation progresses

9.2 Notification Details

Our breach notification will include:

  • Description of the nature of the breach
  • Categories and approximate numbers of affected data subjects
  • Likely consequences of the breach
  • Measures taken or proposed to address the breach
  • Contact point for more information

10. Data Retention and Deletion

10.1 Retention Periods

We retain personal data only as long as:

  • Necessary to provide our services
  • Required by our agreement with you
  • Mandated by applicable law
  • Needed for legitimate business purposes

10.2 Data Deletion

We will delete personal data:

  • Upon termination of our agreement (within 30 days)
  • When you request deletion
  • When retention is no longer necessary
  • As required by applicable law

10.3 Return of Data

Upon request or termination, we will:

  • Return all personal data in a structured format
  • Delete all copies in our possession
  • Ensure sub-processors also delete the data
  • Provide certification of deletion when requested

11. Audits and Compliance

11.1 Audit Rights

You have the right to:

  • Audit our compliance with this DPA
  • Request information about our security measures
  • Review our compliance certifications
  • Conduct on-site inspections (with reasonable notice)

11.2 Compliance Documentation

We will provide:

  • SOC 2 Type II reports
  • ISO 27001 certifications
  • Security assessment reports
  • Compliance attestations

12. Liability and Indemnification

12.1 Liability Allocation

Each party is liable for:

  • Damages caused by its own breach of this DPA
  • Claims arising from its role as Data Controller or Processor
  • Compliance with its obligations under applicable law

12.2 Indemnification

We will indemnify you against claims arising from:

  • Our breach of this DPA
  • Our non-compliance with applicable data protection laws
  • Unauthorized processing by our sub-processors
