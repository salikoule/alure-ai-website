IT Managed Services and Print Business • 5-person sales team • $12M

The Challenge

oeDigital was struggling with its manual calling process. Their 5-person sales team was spending 70% of their time on call preparation and research, leaving little time for actual selling. With only 50 calls per week per rep, they were falling behind their growth targets.

The Solution

oeDigital implemented Alure.ai's complete calling automation platform. Within 30 days, they had AI-powered prospect research, personalized script generation, and automated follow-up sequences running across their entire team.

The AI system analyzed their existing successful call recordings to understand what worked, then generated personalized scripts that maintained their winning approach while scaling to hundreds of prospects daily.

18% Close Rate (vs 5%)
$2M Additional Pipeline
