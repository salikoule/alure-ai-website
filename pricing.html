Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Alure.ai pricing and features

What's included in the free trial? +
Your 14-day free trial includes 10 successful uploads per month, access to basic AI features, and no credit card required. Upgrade anytime to unlock more uploads and advanced features.
Can I change plans anytime? +
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately and we'll prorate any billing differences.
What happens if I exceed my upload limit? +
We'll notify you when you're approaching your limit. You can upgrade your plan to get more successful uploads and additional features.
Do you offer refunds? +
Yes, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied, we'll refund your payment in full, no questions asked.
What integrations do you support? +
We integrate with major CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive), calendars (Google, Outlook), and communication tools (Slack, Teams). Enterprise plans include custom integrations.
