# Product Screenshots

Replace the placeholder images in **`src/assets/images/`** with your actual Alure.ai product screenshots:

- **hero-screenshot.png** (1200×800) – Hero section on homepage. Show your main product/dashboard.
- **demo-script.png** (1000×667) – Script generation / AI personalization screen.
- **demo-analytics.png** (1000×667) – Analytics or reporting dashboard.
