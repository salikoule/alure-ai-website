Our Story

Founded in 2024 by a team of seasoned sales professionals and AI engineers, Alure.ai was born from a simple observation: while digital marketing channels were becoming increasingly saturated and ineffective, cold calling remained the most powerful way to build relationships and close deals.

However, traditional cold calling was broken. Sales reps spent countless hours researching prospects, crafting scripts, and struggling with inconsistent results. We knew there had to be a better way.

That's when we decided to combine the human touch of personal conversation with the power of artificial intelligence to create something revolutionary - a platform that makes every call feel personally crafted while scaling to hundreds of prospects effortlessly.
